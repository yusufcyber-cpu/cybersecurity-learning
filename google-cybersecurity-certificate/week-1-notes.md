## 🔐 Key Concepts

### 1. Confidentiality
Protecting information from unauthorized access.

### 2. Integrity
Ensuring data is accurate and not altered.

### 3. Availability
Making sure systems and data are accessible when needed.

## 🧠 What I Learned
Cybersecurity is not only about hacking, but about protecting systems, people, and data.  
SOC analysts monitor systems, detect threats, and respond to incidents.
